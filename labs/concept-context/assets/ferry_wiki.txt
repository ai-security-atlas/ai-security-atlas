Gull Island Ferries runs one route between the north pier on the mainland and the harbor on Gull Island. The crossing takes about thirty-five minutes in calm weather.
In summer, from May to September, boats leave the north pier every forty minutes from 07:00. The last boat leaves the island at 19:40. In winter the first boat leaves at 08:00 and the last boat leaves the island at 17:10.
Tickets are sold at the ticket office on each pier and on the website. Day returns are cheaper than two single tickets. Season passes are available for island residents and for workers who cross every day.
Children under five travel free. Children under sixteen pay half fare. Students with a valid card get a quarter off any adult ticket. Groups of ten or more can ask the office for a group price.
Bicycles travel free on the passenger deck when there is space. A bicycle with a trailer counts as one cycle booking. Cars, vans and campers must be booked on the vehicle deck at least a day ahead in summer.
Dogs are welcome on the open deck and in the dog area of the lounge. They must stay on a lead. Assistance dogs may go anywhere on board.
Both piers have step-free ramps from the car park. The lift on board goes from the vehicle deck to the passenger lounge. Ask the crew if you need help with a wheelchair or a pram.
The kiosk on board sells sandwiches, soup, hot drinks and ice cream. You may bring your own food. Alcohol is sold only on the evening boats in summer.
If a sailing is delayed by more than one hour, you can rebook for free or ask for a refund at the ticket office. Refunds for weather cancellations go back to the card you paid with within five working days.
Fog is the most common cause of delays in late summer. The harbor master posts updates on the website and on the screens at both piers. Strong winds from the west can cancel the first boats of the day in winter.
The north pier car park has three hundred spaces. Parking is free for the first two hours and then costs a flat day rate. There is a charging point for electric cars next to the ticket office.
Lost property is kept at the island harbor office for thirty days. Call the office or fill in the form on the website with the date and time of your sailing.
The island has a coastal path of twelve kilometers, a small museum about the old lighthouse and two cafes near the harbor. Most visitors walk the path in four to five hours.
Smoking is not allowed anywhere on board, including the open deck. Drones may not be flown from the boat or within the harbor area.
Crew members wear blue jackets with the company gull on the back. In an emergency, follow their instructions and the signs to the muster stations on the passenger deck.
