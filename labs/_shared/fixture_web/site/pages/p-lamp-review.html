Orbit desk lamp review: bright, quiet, one flaw

We used the Orbit desk lamp from Atlas Widgets for three weeks. Its LED module reaches 800 lumens, the dimmer has five steps and the base is heavy enough that the arm never tips.

The flaw: the USB-C port on the base only delivers 5 watts, so it charges a phone slowly.

Verdict: 4 out of 5. A good lamp for a shared office.
