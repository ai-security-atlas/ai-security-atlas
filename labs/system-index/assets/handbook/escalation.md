# Escalation and approvals

## When to escalate
Escalate a ticket to a team lead when a customer threatens legal action or asks for compensation above the refund limit. Escalated tickets get a reply within four working hours.

## Manager approval
Manager approval is recorded in the ticket by the approving manager, never by the agent. An approval given in chat must be copied into the ticket before you act on it.

## Finance sign-off
Finance signs off on invoice refunds and on any payment returned to a different account. Finance replies within one working day.
