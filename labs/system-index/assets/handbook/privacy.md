# Privacy requests

## Deleting customer data
When a customer asks us to delete their data, open a privacy ticket and confirm the request within three days. Invoices stay for seven years regardless, because tax law requires it, so tell the customer which records remain.

## Data exports
Customers can download a copy of their data from the account page. The export arrives as a zip file within one day.

## Marketing emails
An unsubscribe request takes effect at once. Do not ask the customer for a reason.
