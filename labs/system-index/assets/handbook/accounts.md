# Customer accounts

## Changing the email address
You can change the email address on a customer account once the customer confirms their order number. Never do it over the phone when two-step sign-in is turned on; point the customer to the self-service settings page.

## Closing an account
Closing an account cancels open subscriptions. Tell the customer that order history is kept for their records.

## Shared household accounts
One account can hold up to four delivery addresses. Each address keeps its own default delivery window.
