# Warranty and repairs

## Orbit desk lamp
Every Orbit desk lamp carries a two-year warranty, so a faulty lamp is replaced at no charge. That cover ends where a reseller sold the item; send those customers back to the shop where they bought it.

## Cable dock
The Cable dock has a one-year warranty. A dock that stops charging is repaired, not replaced. Ask the customer for the serial number on the underside.

## Proof of purchase
An order number or a receipt photo is enough proof of purchase. Gift recipients can use the gift receipt.
