# Shipping and delivery

## Express shipping
Express orders placed before 2 pm ship the same working day. From 1 December to 24 December the cut-off is 11 am instead, because the warehouse runs a peak schedule.

## Standard shipping
Standard orders ship within two working days. Tracking links are emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse.

## Lost parcels
A parcel counts as lost after ten working days without a tracking update. Reship lost parcels at no charge once the carrier confirms the loss.
