The AI Security Atlas is a **static website**. It has no backend, no user accounts,
and no server-side tracking.

## What is collected

**Nothing, by default.** The site ships with no analytics and sets no tracking
cookies. Pages are served as static files.

- **Theme preference** (light or dark) is stored in your browser's `localStorage`
  and never leaves your device.
- **Search** runs entirely in your browser against a pre-built static index. Your
  queries are not sent to any server.

## Third parties

- Primary-source links point to external sites (papers, documentation, talks).
  Those sites have their own privacy practices.
- If the site operator later enables privacy-friendly analytics, it will be
  configured to anonymize IP addresses and will be disclosed here.

## Hosting

The site is published as static files (for example, via GitHub Pages). Standard
web-server request logs may be retained by the host according to its own policy.
