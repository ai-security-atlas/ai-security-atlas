U-Net-style diffusion backbones are the denoising network commonly used inside image [diffusion generative models](../nodes/arch-diffusion.md). Built from [convolutional networks](../nodes/arch-cnn.md), the U-Net has an encoder path that downsamples toward coarse, low-resolution features and a decoder path that upsamples back to full resolution, with skip connections that reintroduce fine detail from earlier layers. This multiscale design lets the network predict the denoising step while preserving both global structure and local texture. The atlas records both prerequisites: it requires convolutional networks and diffusion.

## Why it matters for security analysis

A U-Net is one realization of the diffusion denoiser, playing the same role that a [diffusion transformer](../nodes/arch-dit.md) fills with attention blocks; the two are distinct backbone choices for the same process. Because its function is iterative denoising rather than autoregressive generation, techniques scoped to token-by-token language models should not be assumed to apply. This node carries no relevant-technique edges of its own, so applicable scope is best inherited from the [diffusion generative models](../nodes/arch-diffusion.md) it serves and from convolutional-model concerns via [convolutional networks](../nodes/arch-cnn.md), evaluated against the concrete pipeline and whether the backbone operates in pixel or latent space. Empirical detail is deferred to the cited literature.
