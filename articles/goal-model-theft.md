This objective covers attacks that appropriate a model itself — either its concrete artifacts (weights, adapters, checkpoints) or its *capabilities* in the form of a functional imitation. The attacker's aim is to walk away with something they can run or resell: a copy of the asset, or a stand-in that behaves closely enough to substitute for it.

The techniques that route here fall along a spectrum from exact theft to behavioral replication. [Unauthorized checkpoint or adapter theft](../nodes/attack-weight-theft.md) takes the artifacts directly. [Partial parameter recovery from API behavior](../nodes/attack-projection-theft.md) reconstructs pieces of the model from what a served interface reveals. [Black-box functional model extraction](../nodes/attack-model-imitation.md) and [unauthorized teacher-student capability transfer](../nodes/attack-distillation-theft.md) instead rebuild the *behavior* by querying and learning from outputs, producing an imitation rather than a byte-for-byte copy.

The distinction worth keeping is between appropriating the exact artifact and replicating what it does. These raise different questions — access and exfiltration controls on one hand, query monitoring and output exposure on the other — even though both end with an attacker holding capability they were not granted. Treat this objective as "did the model or its abilities leave the owner's control," and consult the individual techniques for how each path works.
