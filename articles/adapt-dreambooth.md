DreamBooth personalization is a fine-tuning technique for teaching a diffusion image model a specific subject, such as a particular person, object or pet, so that it can render that subject in new scenes and styles. A small set of reference images is used to bind the subject to an identifier the model can later invoke, while the method tries to preserve the model's broader generative ability. The approach is introduced in *DreamBooth: Fine Tuning Text-to-Image Diffusion Models for Subject-Driven Generation*.

The technique requires a [diffusion generative model](../nodes/arch-diffusion.md) as its base and proceeds through [supervised fine-tuning](../nodes/life-sft.md), updating the model on the reference images against a subject-driven objective. This places it among adaptation methods that specialize a general model toward a narrow target.

DreamBooth is presented here as a neutral personalization technique, and within this dataset it carries no direct association with a specific attack. Its typical uses are creative and commercial: consistent depictions of a subject across generated images, custom avatars and product imagery. Details of the training loss, regularization to avoid overfitting the subject, and the number of reference images involved are left to the cited source.
