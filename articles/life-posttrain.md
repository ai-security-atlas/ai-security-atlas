Post-training is the umbrella lifecycle stage covering adaptation after pretraining — everything done to a base model to make it more useful, including but not limited to alignment. It requires [pretraining](../nodes/life-pretrain.md): post-training presupposes a base model that has already learned broad patterns, and reshapes that model toward particular behaviors, formats or objectives.

As an umbrella, it encompasses more specific stages rather than naming a single technique. [Supervised fine-tuning](../nodes/life-sft.md) and [preference data and reward models](../nodes/life-preferences.md) both sit under it, and alignment work is one part of the space it covers, not its whole extent. This breadth is deliberate: treating post-training as a category preserves the distinction between "further adaptation" in general and any one method that performs it.

Security-relevant scope at this level is inherited by the concrete stages it contains. Because post-training modifies a model's behavior after its broad capabilities are set, whether a base model's original properties still hold afterward is something to evaluate rather than assume — a theme that recurs in the alignment and safety stages beneath it. The cited instruction-following and preference-optimization works describe representative post-training routes; consult them for the specifics of each.
