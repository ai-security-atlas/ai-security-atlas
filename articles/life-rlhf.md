RLHF (reinforcement learning from human feedback) and policy optimization is a post-training route that adjusts a model's behavior using feedback, typically by fitting a reward model to human or synthetic preferences and then optimizing the model's policy against that reward. It requires [preference data and reward models](../nodes/life-preferences.md) as its input signal.

This is not the only alignment route. [Direct Preference Optimization](../nodes/life-dpo.md) reaches a related goal from the same preference data without a separate reward-model-plus-policy-optimization loop. RLHF's distinguishing feature is the explicit reward model and the reinforcement-learning step that optimizes against it; knowing whether a model was aligned this way or through a more direct objective is useful when reasoning about how its behavior was produced.

Security-relevant scope follows the dependency on preferences and a reward model. Because the optimized policy is shaped by the reward signal, the integrity and sourcing of the underlying comparisons — and of the reward model derived from them — are relevant to the resulting behavior, matching the scope concerns of the preference stage it builds on. The cited instruction-following work describes RLHF in practice; defer to it for empirical results rather than treating alignment outcomes as guaranteed.
