This objective is deliberately an adjacent lens rather than a compromise category. It captures cases where an attacker abuses an AI system's *legitimate capability* to amplify harmful activity, without necessarily breaking into the model, corrupting its training, or subverting its host. The system may be working exactly as built; the harm comes from what that working capability is pointed at.

Because the mechanism is use rather than intrusion, only one technique routes here: [AI-assisted harmful activity](../nodes/attack-capability-abuse.md), which this objective both is reached through and is a prerequisite for. That pairing reflects the framing — misuse is defined by the objective and realized through capability abuse, rather than through a spread of distinct exploits against the model or its environment.

Keep this partition separate in your mental model from objectives like violating behavioral constraints or causing unauthorized actions. Those turn on defeating a safeguard or a permission boundary; misuse can occur even when every safeguard holds and every action is authorized, simply because a capable tool lowers the effort of doing harm. It is an editorial boundary meant to keep that "amplification" case visible, not an exhaustive taxonomy. For how capability abuse is characterized, follow the linked technique.
