Differentially private training bounds how much any single training example can influence the resulting model, under a stated privacy definition and an explicit accounting procedure. It operates on [losses, gradients and optimization](../nodes/concept-gradients.md): during training, per-example gradient contributions are clipped and calibrated noise is added, and a privacy accountant tracks the cumulative budget spent. The guarantee is mathematical — it limits what can be inferred about the presence or content of individual records, rather than relying on heuristics.

As a mitigation it is conditional on the budget chosen. It can reduce [language-model training-data extraction](../nodes/attack-training-extraction.md) and [membership inference](../nodes/attack-membership.md) by weakening the link between any one record and the model's outputs, with the strength of protection set by the privacy parameters. Residual risk and trade-offs are real: looser budgets protect less, tighter budgets and the added noise typically cost model utility, and the guarantee holds only for the unit and assumptions the accounting actually covers. It constrains per-example leakage, not every privacy failure.

Unlike [training-data minimization](../nodes/defense-data-hygiene.md), which reduces exposure without a formal claim, this control offers a quantifiable privacy property. Consult the cited work on deep learning with differential privacy for the mechanism and its accounting.
